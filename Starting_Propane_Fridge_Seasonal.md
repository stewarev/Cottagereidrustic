# Starting Your Propane Refrigerator at the Beginning of Season

A comprehensive guide to safely bringing your UNIQUE propane refrigerator online for the season.

## Pre-Startup Safety Checks

Before igniting the fridge, perform these essential safety verifications:

### Smell Test
- Open windows to ensure adequate ventilation
- Approach the refrigerator and smell around all connections
- **If you detect any gas odor:**
  1. Do not attempt to light the appliance
  2. Extinguish any open flames
  3. Do not touch electrical switches
  4. Evacuate the building
  5. Call emergency services

### Ventilation Inspection
- Verify the refrigerator is installed in a well-ventilated space
- Confirm clearances are maintained:
  - Top: 6 inches
  - Sides: 2 inches  
  - Rear: 1 inch (minimum for airflow over cooling unit)
- Ensure no obstructions block the cooling fins at the rear

## Pre-Startup Mechanical Checks

### Level Verification
- **Critical Step:** Use a bubble level to check that the refrigerator is level both side-to-side and front-to-back
- Improper leveling prevents the absorption cooling system from functioning and may void warranty
- Adjust feet as needed to achieve level positioning

### Gas Connection Inspection
- Check all gas line connections for any visible damage or corrosion
- Using a non-corrosive leak detector or soap solution, inspect connections for leaks
- **Never use a flame** to check for leaks
- Ensure propane tank is properly connected and valve is in good condition
- Confirm tank has adequate propane supply

### Power Supply (if using 110V mode)
- Verify the outlet is grounded
- Check the power cord for any damage
- Confirm adequate ventilation around the unit

## Startup Procedure - Gas Mode (Electronic Ignition)

1. **Turn thermostat knob** to maximum (#4 setting)
2. **Select gas mode** by turning selector button to "Gas"
3. **Push and hold** the ignition button (B)
4. **While holding button B, push and hold** the control button (D) until the flame sensor needle (C) moves into the green area on the gauge
5. **Release button B**, but continue holding button D for another 10-15 seconds
6. **Release button D** - the burner should now be lit and operational

## Startup Procedure - 110V Electric Mode

1. **Plug into grounded outlet** (if not permanently installed)
2. **Turn selector switch** to AC position
3. **Turn thermostat knob** to maximum setting

## Cool Down & Stabilization

- **Allow 8 hours minimum** with doors closed before loading any food
- Keep the thermostat set to maximum during this period
- The system needs time to establish proper cooling temperatures
- After 24 hours of operation, adjust thermostat to desired setting
- Do not expect food to be fully chilled until the complete cool-down period is finished

## Initial Food Loading

- Wait until the 8-hour cool-down period is complete
- Check that interior temperature is properly cold before storing perishables
- Fill gradually rather than loading completely at once
- Proper spacing allows air circulation

## First Season Maintenance

- Schedule a professional periodic inspection to verify:
  - Proper gas pressure (should be 11" water column)
  - Clean burner and flue system
  - No obstructions or corrosion
- Monitor the burner flame - it should be steady and blue
- Keep the area around the cooling unit clean and unobstructed

## Ongoing Monitoring

- Check the refrigerator daily during the first week of operation for any issues
- Monitor for unusual sounds or smells
- Ensure the flame remains steady (if using gas mode)
- Watch temperature to ensure it's cooling properly
