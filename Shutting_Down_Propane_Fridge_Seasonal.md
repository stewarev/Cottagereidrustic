# Shutting Down Your Propane Refrigerator at the End of Season

A step-by-step guide to safely winterize and prepare your UNIQUE propane refrigerator for seasonal storage.

## Pre-Shutdown Preparation

### Empty the Refrigerator
- Remove all food items
- Discard perishables that won't store through the off-season
- Clean out any spilled items or debris

### Defrost the Freezer Section
- Allow 24 hours for the freezer to defrost naturally
- **Never use sharp objects** to remove ice - they can damage the cooling fins
- Wipe the freezer section manually with a soft cloth
- **Note:** There is no drain in the freezer section, so catch meltwater with towels or a pan as it drips
- Continue until all frost is removed
- Place towels inside to absorb any remaining moisture

## Cleaning & Maintenance

### Interior Cleaning
- Wipe down all interior surfaces with a damp cloth
- Clean shelves and removable parts
- Ensure all food residue is removed
- Allow interior to air dry completely

### Burner and Flue System (Gas Mode)
- If using propane, have the burner and flue system professionally inspected and cleaned
- A clean system is critical for safe operation and prevents carbon monoxide buildup
- Remove any debris, dust, or corrosion from the burner area

### Orifice Cleaning (if needed)
- If the burner fails to light properly next season, the orifice may need cleaning
- Soak the orifice in alcohol and blow clean with compressed air
- **Never use a pin or wire** - this will damage the delicate opening

## Shutdown Sequence

### For Gas Mode Operation

1. **Turn thermostat knob** to OFF/minimum setting
2. **Turn selector button** from "Gas" to OFF position
3. **Allow burner to cool** for 5-10 minutes
4. **Shut off the propane tank valve** by turning clockwise until snug (do not force)
5. **Disconnect the gas line** if you're removing the appliance for storage (optional - only if the tank is being removed)

### For 110V Electric Mode

1. **Turn thermostat knob** to OFF/minimum setting
2. **Turn selector switch** to OFF position
3. **Unplug from outlet** or switch off the circuit breaker

## Pre-Storage Inspection

- Check all exterior surfaces for rust or corrosion
- Wipe down the exterior with a dry cloth
- Ensure the door seals are clean and in good condition
- Check for any visible damage or leaks

## Storage & Protection

### If Refrigerator Remains in Place
- Leave the door slightly ajar to allow air circulation and prevent odor/mold
- Place an open container of baking soda inside to absorb odors
- Ensure the space remains well-ventilated
- Cover the exterior with a breathable cover if exposed to elements
- Check monthly to ensure no moisture accumulation

### If Refrigerator is Being Moved
- Ensure the unit is level before disconnecting (prevents fluid displacement in cooling system)
- Disconnect gas line securely and cap both ends
- Do not lay the refrigerator on its side or back - this can damage the absorption system
- Cover to protect from dust and moisture during transport
- Store in a dry, well-ventilated location if possible

## End-of-Season Checklist

- [ ] Food removed and cleaned
- [ ] Freezer defrosted completely
- [ ] Interior cleaned and dried
- [ ] Exterior cleaned
- [ ] Thermostat set to OFF
- [ ] Gas mode: Propane tank valve shut off
- [ ] Electric mode: Power disconnected
- [ ] Gas connections secured (if applicable)
- [ ] Door left ajar (if remaining in place) or sealed (if in storage)
- [ ] Odor absorber placed inside (if remaining in place)
- [ ] Unit covered if exposed to weather

## Before Next Season Startup

When you're ready to bring the refrigerator back online:
- Ensure the unit is level before starting
- Verify gas line connections for leaks (if applicable)
- Consult the **Starting Your Propane Refrigerator at the Beginning of Season** guide
- Have a professional perform a pre-season inspection if the unit has been in storage
