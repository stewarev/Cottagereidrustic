## Safety and Warnings

### **FIRE OR EXPLOSION HAZARD**
**If you smell gas:**
1. Open windows [cite: 12, 119].
2. Do not attempt to light the appliance; ensure it is in the OFF position [cite: 13].
3. Extinguish any open flame [cite: 14, 121].
4. Do not touch electrical switches [cite: 15, 120].
5. Do not use electronic devices (cell phones/landlines) [cite: 16, 17].
6. Evacuate the building or recreational vehicle [cite: 18].
7. Shut off fuel supply at the LP tank [cite: 18].
8. Call emergency services [cite: 19, 122].

### **CARBON MONOXIDE POISONING**
* This is an unvented gas-fired appliance that uses oxygen from the area where it is used [cite: 25].
* Adequate combustion and ventilation air must be provided [cite: 26].
* Unsafe levels of CO can kill you. Symptoms include headaches, running nose (mild), dizziness, vomiting (medium), and brain damage or death (extreme) [cite: 57, 70, 71, 72].
* **CO Alarming Device:** If the alarm sounds, immediately move to fresh air and call emergency services [cite: 60, 64, 67].

## Chapter 2: Installation

### Location and Ventilation
* Ensure free air circulation over the cooling unit at the rear [cite: 142].
* Do not place the refrigerator in a restricted space [cite: 144].
* **Clearances:**
    * Top: 6" [cite: 156]
    * Sides: 2" [cite: 157]
    * Rear: 1" [cite: 158]
* **Flooring:** Do not install directly on carpeting. Use a metal or wood panel for protection [cite: 160].

### Gas Connection
* Refrigerator must be installed by a licensed gas fitter [cite: 40].
* Propane supply pressure must be set at 11" water column [cite: 41].
* Check all connections for leaks using a non-corrosive leak detector or soap and water. **DO NOT USE A FLAME** [cite: 186, 187].

### Leveling
* The refrigerator must be level both side-to-side and front-to-back using a bubble level [cite: 50, 193].
* Proper leveling is critical for the absorption refrigeration system to function; failure to do so can block cooling and void the warranty [cite: 211, 212].

## Chapter 3: General Operating Instructions

### Gas Start-Up Procedure (Electronic Ignition)
1. Turn thermostat knob (E) to maximum (#4) [cite: 221].
2. Turn selector button (G) to "Gas" [cite: 222].
3. Push and hold button (B), then push and hold button (D) until the flame sensor needle (C) moves into the green area [cite: 222].
4. Release (B) but hold (D) for another 10-15 seconds before releasing [cite: 223].

### 110V Operation
1. Plug into a grounded outlet [cite: 232].
2. Turn selector switch (G) to AC position [cite: 233].
3. Turn thermostat knob (E) to maximum [cite: 234].

## Chapter 4: Maintenance & Service
* **Cleaning:** The burner and flue system must be kept clean to prevent CO build-up [cite: 77, 129].
* **Periodic Inspection:** Recommended once or twice a year [cite: 333].
* **Orifice Cleaning:** Soak in alcohol and blow out with compressed air. **DO NOT** use a pin or wire as it will damage the opening [cite: 366, 370].

## Chapter 5: Troubleshooting
If the refrigerator does not cool, check if:
1. The burner orifice is clogged [cite: 389].
2. The refrigerator is not level [cite: 390].
3. There is heavy frost build-up on fins [cite: 392].
4. The LP gas pressure is low (should be 11" W.C.) [cite: 396].

## Chapter 7: Temperature Controls & Food Storage
* **Cool Down Period:** Operate with doors closed for at least 8 hours before loading food [cite: 438].
* **Initial Setting:** Start on "MAX". After 24 hours, adjust as needed [cite: 447, 448].
* **Defrosting:** Defrost when frost exceeds 1/2" thickness [cite: 284]. Wipe the freezer section manually as there is no drain [cite: 297].
